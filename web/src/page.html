    Boo Who?

    Light the stranger. Find the clues. Hold the gate.

    3D models by Quaternius (CC0)

    How to play

    Hold the gate for 5 nights

    1. A hooded stranger walks to your gate. Shine your lantern on it.
    2. Hold the light on a body part for a second to find a clue: face, eyes, hands, hair or shadow. Turn the lantern off to see its aura.
    3. Each clue turns over the monster cards it rules out. The cards left show how likely each one is.
    4. Click the monster you think it is (keys 1 to 5). Fewer clues, more points.
      Zombie · shovel
      Witch · holy water
      Ghost · salt
      Vampire · garlic stake
      Mummy · fire torch
    5. Wrong, or too late: the gate loses 1 of its 5 lanterns. Lose all 5 and you lose.

    Stuck? Ask the Owl (key O) where to look. The lantern burns oil, so turn it off when you are not searching.
