    How to play

    Hold the gate for 5 nights

    1. A hooded monster walks toward the gate.
    2. Six clues appear as it comes closer. Blood and rot, the best clues, come last.
    3. Pick its weapon (keys 1 to 5). The earlier you pick, the more coins you earn.
      ⛏️ Shovel · Zombie
      💧 Holy water · Witch
      🧂 Salt circle · Ghost
      🧄 Garlic stake · Vampire
      🔥 Fire torch · Mummy
    4. Unsure? Pay the Owl (key O). It sees only the clues you see.
    5. Wrong weapon or too late: the gate loses 1 of its 5 lanterns. Lose all 5 and you lose.
